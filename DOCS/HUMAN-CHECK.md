# Human check: 15 minutes of a person using it

Run this after two robot review rounds. AI review has a ceiling: a third round
finds less and less, and none of them can feel confused. A person can.

## Who

Someone who didn't build it and doesn't know how it should work. Not you, not the
agent, not the person who wrote the spec. One person is enough.

## Setup

- A real device (the phone, if it's a phone product), not a resized window.
- Real data, or as close as you can get. Not "Test 1, Test 2".
- You sit next to them. You don't explain anything first.

## The tasks

Write 3 to 5, each as a goal and not as steps:

1. [e.g., "Sign up and find your first result"]
2. [e.g., "Change something you got wrong"]
3. [e.g., "Undo or leave, and come back"]

Hand over one task at a time. **No help.** If they ask "what do I press?", write
that down and say "what would you try?".

## What to observe

- **Where they hesitate:** the pause before a tap.
- **What they misread:** a label, an icon, an error message taken the wrong way.
- **What they expected:** the thing they tried that wasn't there.
- Anything they say out loud. Don't defend the product.
## What to write down

One line per observation, as it happened, not as a conclusion:

> [task 2]: looked at "Save" for 6s, tapped "Back" instead, lost the form.

## After

Every observation becomes a **card or a fix**. If it becomes neither, delete it.
Fix before the next round of reviews, not after, then run this again with a
different person.
